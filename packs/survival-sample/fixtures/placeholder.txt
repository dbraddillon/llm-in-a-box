Basic wound care: clean the area with water, apply direct pressure to stop
bleeding, and cover with a clean dressing. Seek professional medical care when
available.

Water purification: boiling water for at least one minute kills most pathogens.
At altitudes above 6,500 feet, boil for three minutes instead.

Shelter priority order commonly taught for survival situations: protection from
the elements first, then signaling for rescue, then water, then fire, then food.

Layering for cold weather: a moisture-wicking base layer, an insulating mid
layer, and a wind/water resistant outer shell. Avoid cotton next to skin in cold,
wet conditions since it loses insulating value when damp.
